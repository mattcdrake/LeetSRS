# LeetSRS

Spaced repetition for LeetCode practice.

**Problem / card**: A problem is a LeetCode question; a card is the learner's study record for it.

**Review / revisit**: Every rating is a review. Ratings after the first are revisits; repetition counts include the first.

**Practiced problem**: Rated at least once, including paused cards.

**Lapse**: An Again rating on a revisit, counted cumulatively.

**Daily new-card allowance**: Limits first reviews admitted to the queue, not cards added. Uses local calendar days.

**Problem / memory difficulty**: LeetCode's Easy/Medium/Hard classification versus the scheduler's estimated difficulty of strengthening a memory.

## Catalog and topic analysis (agreed direction)

**Catalog / topic**: Shared problem metadata and its subject tags. One problem can belong to several topics.

**Topic coverage**: Practiced-problem counts across curated interview topics, not catalog-completion progress.

**Topic attention**: Retention difficulty, isolated or widespread across practiced problems. Being overdue alone does not qualify.

**Review concentration**: Accumulated revisits alongside distinct practiced-problem counts; activity, not evidence of difficulty.
